Project Summary

Visual Analysis in Tableau

These four projects use different visual formats for different questions. The NYC work compares property sales and unit mix using tables and a borough map. The World Bank story compares potential markets for a rail-booking startup. The stock-market dashboard brings trading volume, daily prices, and cumulative returns together for historical analysis.

Skills and What I Learned

  • Tableau
  • Calculated fields
  • Maps
  • Dashboards
  • Data storytelling

I learned to choose visual formats around the decision a reader needs to make, use calculated measures for comparisons, and arrange worksheets into a story or dashboard that readers can explore.

New York City Real Estate · Projects 1A and 1B

Project 1A

Build Custom Tables with NYC Real Estate Data

I prepared a year of NYC property sales data and built reporting views for average sale price per square foot, sales by date, and the residential and commercial unit mix.

View the residential and commercial unit comparison

Skills. Calculated fields · Aggregation · Table formatting

What I learned. I learned to define comparable measures and summarize property sales at a useful level of detail.

Project 1B

Create Data Visualizations with NYC Real Estate Data

I mapped average sale price per square foot by borough so readers could compare geographic patterns using a common unit of measure. A borough average is a starting point for comparing markets. Neighborhood and property-type breakdowns would provide more detail for an investment decision.

View the borough price-per-square-foot map

Skills. Tableau maps · Geographic comparisons · Data types

What I learned. I learned to use geography and a common price-per-square-foot measure to help readers compare locations.

NYC Rolling Property Sales

The source file contains property transaction records, including borough, building class, unit counts, square footage, sale price, and sale date.

World Bank and Stock Market · Projects 2 and 3

Project 2

Build a Tableau Story with World Bank Data

I built a Tableau story to evaluate where a rail-booking startup could launch. I used World Development Indicators to compare railway infrastructure and passenger activity alongside GDP and the business environment. The story gives readers a way to weigh transportation demand and market conditions together.

Open the World Bank Tableau story

Skills. Tableau stories · Indicators · Stakeholder communication

What I learned. I learned to connect several indicators into a decision-oriented narrative.

Project 3

Create Advanced Visualizations with Stock Market Data

I joined historical daily stock prices to S&P 500 ticker reference data and combined trading volume, candlestick charts, and cumulative returns in one dashboard. The views give readers several ways to explore the same historical trading data.

Open the stock market dashboard

Skills. Data joins · Candlestick charts · Dashboard design

What I learned. I learned how complementary views of volume, prices, and returns help readers explore historical trading data.
