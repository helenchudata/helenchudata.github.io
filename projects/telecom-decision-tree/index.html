Project Summary

I one-hot encoded categorical features from Cell2Cell telecom data, split the data for training and testing, and compared decision trees with different depths and leaf sizes.

scikit-learn · Decision trees · One-hot encoding · Accuracy

Skills and What I Learned

  • scikit-learn
  • Decision trees
  • One-hot encoding
  • Model evaluation

I learned how categorical encoding makes features usable by a classifier and how tree depth and leaf size affect generalization. The deeper tree performed worse on the saved test split.

Results and Interpretation

The displayed runs show test accuracy of 71.09% at depth 8 and 62.06% at depth 32. The notebook’s written analysis reports about 71.45% with depth 4 and a minimum leaf size of 20. The results illustrate how deeper trees can generalize less effectively.
