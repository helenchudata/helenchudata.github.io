4. EXPLORE CHURN HYPOTHESES

1. Question: Do younger customers churn at a higher rate?

Hypothesis: Younger customers are always looking for new technology and better deals, while older customers might be less inclined to switch companies from what they are familiar with.

joined_df['age_group'] = pd.cut(joined_df['age'], bins=[0, 30, 60, 100], labels=['Under 30', '30-60', 'Over 60'])

plt.figure(figsize=(6,4))
sns.countplot(data=joined_df, x='age_group', hue='churn_label')
plt.title('Churn Rate by Age Group')
plt.show()

Plot Description: This shows that customers that fall in the age group 30-60 are most likely to churn higher. Under 30 is least likely to.

plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
sns.boxplot(data=joined_df, x='churn_label', y='age')
plt.title('Box Plot of Age by Churn Status')
plt.xlabel('Churn Status')
plt.ylabel('Age')
plt.show()

Plot Description: Customer median age to churn is 50 years old.

2. Question: Does Type of Internet Service Affect Churn?

Hypothesis: Customers with no internet service are more likely to chrun.

plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
sns.countplot(x='internet_service', hue='churn_label', data=joined_df)
plt.title('Churn Rate by Internet Service')
plt.show()

Plot Description: Customers with internet service are less likely to churn when they have internet service. 

plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
sns.countplot(x='internet_type', hue='churn_label', data=joined_df)
plt.title('Churn Rate by Internet Type')
plt.show()

Plot Description: Customers who have fiber optic as their internet are more likely to churn higher than the other internet types.

3. Question: Does contract type affect churn?

Hypothesis: Customers with month-to-month contracts have a higher churn rate than to those with longer-term contracts.

plt.figure(figsize=(6,4))
sns.countplot(data=joined_df, x='contract', hue='churn_label')
plt.title('Churn Rate by Contract Type')
plt.show()

Plot Description: Customers with month-to-month contract have higher churn rate. 

plt.figure(figsize=(6,4))
sns.boxplot(data=joined_df, x='contract', y='monthly_charge', hue='churn_label')
plt.title('Monthly Charge by Contract Type')
plt.show()

Plot Description: The higher the monthly charge per contract type are likely to churn higher.
