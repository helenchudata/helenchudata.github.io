Project Summary

I compared a stacked regression ensemble with linear regression, a decision tree, gradient boosting, and random forest on the notebook’s transformed Airbnb price target.

Stacking · Regression · Random forest · RMSE · R²

Skills and What I Learned

  • Regression

I learned how an ensemble combines different regressors and how to compare it against individual baselines. Reporting the target scale is essential when interpreting RMSE.

Results and Interpretation

The stacked model recorded RMSE of 0.6196 and R² of 0.6364. Random forest was close at RMSE 0.6280 and R² 0.6265. These RMSE values are on the modeled target scale and should be interpreted in that scale.
