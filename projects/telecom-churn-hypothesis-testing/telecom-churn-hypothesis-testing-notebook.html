under_30_gb = df.groupby(['under_30', 'churn_label']).customer_id.count().unstack()

2B. State Your Hypotheses

Null Hypothesis: There is no difference in churn rate between customers under 30 and customers 30 or older

Alternative Hypothesis: Customers under 30 churn at a higher rate than those 30 and over

2C. Perform the Hypothesis Test

successes = [under_30_gb.loc['Yes', 'Yes'], under_30_gb.loc['No', 'Yes']]
nobs = [under_30_gb.loc['Yes', 'total_count'], under_30_gb.loc['No', 'total_count']]

z_stat, p_value = proportions_ztest(successes, nobs)

print(f"Z-statistic: {z_stat}")
print(f"P-value: {p_value}")
Z-statistic: -4.5733014216894095
P-value: 4.800986893226653e-06

Description of findings: There is a significant difference in churn rate between customers under 30 than those 30 and over because the p-value is greater than 0.05. Reject the null hypthoesis. However, customers 30 and over have a higher chrun rate of 27.7% than under 30 which is 21.7%.
