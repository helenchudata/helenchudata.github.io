Project Summary

I used two-proportion z-tests to compare churn across age groups, unlimited-data plans, and fiber-optic service. I combined subgroup rates with test results to assess which differences warranted further study.

Skills and What I Learned

  • Python
  • pandas
  • statsmodels
  • Hypothesis testing
  • Proportion tests
  • Statistical interpretation

I learned to compare effect sizes alongside p-values and to check whether an observed difference supports the direction of the original hypothesis. The age result went in the opposite direction. The tests establish differences in this sample, while further research is needed to explain their causes.

Key Findings

Customers aged 30 or older had a churn rate of 27.7%, compared with 21.7% for younger customers. Rates were 31.6% with unlimited data versus 16.0% without, and 40.7% with fiber optic versus 15.8% without. All three recorded two-sided tests returned p-values below 0.05.

The reported tests use the two-sided default. The portfolio interpretation follows those tests and the recorded p-values.
