Telecom Churn Hypothesis Testing

I used two-proportion z-tests to compare churn across age groups, unlimited-data plans, and fiber-optic service. I combined subgroup rates with test results to assess which differences warranted further study.

  • Python
  • pandas
  • statsmodels
  • Hypothesis testing
  • Proportion tests
  • Statistical interpretation

What I learned. I learned to compare effect sizes alongside p-values and to check whether an observed difference supports the direction of the original hypothesis. The age result went in the opposite direction. The tests establish differences in this sample, while further research is needed to explain their causes.
